    The daily loop

    Run this every single day for 90 days. That is the whole secret.

    1. Ask (10 min): send 5 pitches / applications / messages. Income follows volume of asks.
    2. Build (30 min): work on the asset that compounds — a gig, a video, a product, a portfolio.
    3. Publish (10 min): ship something visible. Unpublished work earns nothing.
    4. Log (2 min): record money in, money out, and what you learned.
    5. Review (5 min weekly): kill what's dead, double what converts.

    The honest math of "free daily money"

    Reality
    • Surveys & microtasks: ₹20–150/day. Real money, tiny ceiling.
    • MLM / "join for ₹499" schemes: you are the customer, not the earner.
    • "Trading bots / doubling apps": the fastest way to lose ₹10,000.
    Workable
    • Skill services: ₹500–5,000/day once you have 3 paying clients.
    • Local services: cash in 24 hours, no platform needed.
    • Reselling / affiliate: ₹200–2,000/day with traffic.
    Compounding
    • Content + digital products: near-zero marginal cost per sale.
    • A website of your own: the one asset nobody can deplatform.
    • Email list: 1,000 subscribers can out-earn 100,000 followers.

      1. Who is paying, and why? If you can't name the customer, there is no business.
      2. Do I have to pay to start? Legitimate work pays you. It doesn't invoice you.
      3. Does it work without recruiting others? If income depends on sign-ups, it's a pyramid.
